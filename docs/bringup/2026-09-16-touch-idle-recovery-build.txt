Touch idle-packet recovery build
================================

The flashed recovery image reached the Brookesia Display service but later
aborted in `Display::read_touch()` while formatting repeated warnings for the
CST9220 idle packet (`ack=0x06`). The board touch adapter now treats
`ESP_ERR_INVALID_RESPONSE` as an empty sample, while preserving other I2C
transport errors as failures.

Validation: incremental ESP-IDF build passed. App image size is 0x4519b0 bytes;
46% of the app partition remains free. No flash performed for this build.
