# Weather picture prompts

These images were generated with the built-in ImageGen tool. Each prompt used
the common specification below and one condition-specific request.

## Common specification

```text
Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: 128 x 128 weather picture for a small square AMOLED smartwatch display
Scene/backdrop: genuinely transparent background
Style/medium: polished soft 3D illustration, tactile glass-and-clay materials, clean premium smartwatch UI art
Composition/framing: centered, front-facing, fill about 90 percent of the square, strong silhouette, readable at 128 pixels
Lighting/mood: high contrast on black AMOLED
Constraints: isolated weather subject only, actual alpha transparency, no circle container, no border, no text, no numbers, no watermark
Avoid: flat glyph, line icon, landscape, horizon, tiny details, cropped subject
```

## Condition-specific requests

- Clear: One large warm golden sun with a soft amber center, crisp rounded rays,
  subtle cyan rim light, and gentle volumetric glow.
- Few clouds: A large warm golden sun partly covered by one small soft white
  cloud, with subtle cyan rim light and gentle glow.
- Scattered clouds: Two separated soft white and pale-blue clouds with visible
  depth and a small patch of blue light between them.
- Broken clouds: A full cluster of layered gray, silver, and pale-blue clouds,
  dense but luminous, with no sun visible.
- Shower rain: A dimensional blue-gray cloud with several large bright cyan
  raindrops falling beneath it, energetic but clean.
- Rain: A dark blue-gray cloud, a partly hidden warm sun behind one edge, and
  several long glossy blue raindrops falling beneath it.
- Thunderstorm: A deep charcoal storm cloud with one large vivid
  electric-yellow lightning bolt and a few blue raindrops.
- Snow: A soft pale-blue winter cloud with three large crystalline white
  snowflakes falling beneath it, frosted highlights, and clean spacing.
- Mist: Several layered horizontal ribbons of translucent pearl-white and
  pale-blue fog, softly curved and floating, with clear depth.
